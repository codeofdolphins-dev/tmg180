TMG180 · Worker phase reference

The Worker Workspace

A self-employed worker's own space: their standing, their evidence, their directory listing. Everything about a participant is borrowed under consent and returned the moment it's revoked — the worker records what happened, never who the participant is.

Workspace built · 8 slices live 4 consent permissions WCPS Layer A never shared 25 Aug canon reshapes the notes

Non-negotiables · worker-facing

Not an employer. No assigning, ranking, rostering or supervising. Workers stay self-employed with their own ABN and their own clients.

Layer A never leaves. The worker's private narrative is serialised in their own detail view and nowhere else — not to participants, not to admin.

Consent gates writes, not memory. Every write about a participant needs an active grant; reading a worker's own past logs never does.

Voice and observation stay apart. What the participant said is stored separately from what the worker observed — never merged.

Submitted is final. A locked log or approved snapshot is never edited — corrections are appended as addenda.

Standing is a count, not a grade. "N of M in order." Never a rating, a score, or a ranking against other workers.

Acknowledgement follows reading. Confirming happens on the item's own page, after the document has been opened. Append-only per version.

Workers never write the Profile. They may read it under consent; the participant's source answers are theirs alone.

What the 25 Aug canon changes for workers

The Longitudinal package rewrites the notes and the Profile relationship. Nothing here is built yet — it follows the participant phase, because the worker's read view needs the participant's grant model to exist first.

New Daily Case Note

A different, checkbox-led form: what was part of today · what support helped · how today compared with usual · what it made possible. Completable without writing narrative when the boxes describe the day.

Replaces the current field set · DN-01

Participant voice, stored apart

A collaboration state ("we completed this together" / "participant chose not to contribute") plus their own words — structurally separate from worker observation.

DN-02

New Monthly Case Note

"My Month" — 13 sections of shared reflection. Fluctuation, effort and recovery, what was maintained. Improvement is never required as an outcome.

MN-02

Profile boundary, enforced

Monthly can flag "a Personal Profile module may need updating" — that raises a review prompt for the participant. It must never edit the Profile.

MN-01

Module-scoped read view

Profile access stops being one boolean: the participant grants the full profile or selected modules, and ungranted modules must be unreachable.

PP-08 worker half

Provenance per statement

Every recorded line carries its origin — participant report, participant quote, worker observation, worker record, professional evidence.

Open flags · worker

  1. Calendar has nothing behind it — sessions aren't a table, and rostering is banned by design. Decide what it shows, or drop it.
