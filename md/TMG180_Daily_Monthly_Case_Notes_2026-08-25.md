**TMG180 monthly recording approach** — The Monthly Note is a shared reflection, not a judgement of the participant. Record what actually happened across the month: what worked, what remained difficult, what changed, what support made possible and what the participant wants understood. Improvement is not the only meaningful outcome. Maintaining health, abilities, independence, relationships, routines, stability and an ordinary life can also matter. Use respectful everyday language. Don't diagnose, speculate about motivation or turn ordinary human responses into clinical labels. Where the participant's experience differs from the worker's observation, record both rather than deciding which version is "correct."
