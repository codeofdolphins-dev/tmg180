## 1. Executive summary

The file is close on content — almost every canonical screen exists and the platform's core principles (no matching, no booking, participant ownership) are respected in the flows. But it is **not internally consistent**, and several items the documentation treats as settled are still open in the file:

1. **The side navigation is the single biggest problem.** One product should have 3 sidebars (one per portal). The file has **22 distinct sidebar variants**: 7 across the 17 participant frames, 6 portal-name variants across the 14 worker frames, and **9 variants across the 13 admin frames**. Items appear, disappear, and get renamed frame to frame; three pages aren't in any sidebar at all; two frames highlight the wrong item; sign-out exists on fewer than half the frames and is labelled three different ways. → Section 4.
2. **Banned terminology is still drawn on ~17 frames**, including `"TMG180 Governance Admin"` as a card title on two chooser screens and as the **admin sidebar's own label** on two more, and `"Personal Profile (FCA Baseline)"` on the participant dashboard — the exact strings the Final Override's acceptance tests search for. → Section 5.
3. **7+ screens the docs require have no frame at all** — most urgently **Create Account** (sign-up is already being built in code with no design reference) and the **Participant Check-in** (two frames have "Start Check-in" buttons that lead to a screen that doesn't exist). → Section 2.
4. **Duplicates and competing frames**: two "Choose Your Workspace" frames plus a third chooser (Role Selection), two different set-new-password screens with different password rules, pre-override and post-override worker directory/profile frames coexisting, and three separate availability editors. → Section 3.
5. **The two deletions promised in the Design Alignment Summary were never done.** "Participant Overview" is still a nav item on **10 of 13 admin frames** and a full page; the availability block is still on the worker profile (and duplicated twice more). → Section 6.
6. **The My Personal Profile section list disagrees with the Final Override seed** — and also disagrees with itself (hub cards vs section pages vs frame names give up to three names for one section; one hub card has no page; one page is missing from the hub). → Section 6, R-01.
7. **Participant-feature empty states wear worker-portal sidebars** (No Favourites, No Export), and the "Link Expired" screen's copy is about snapshot share-links, not password resets — the flow that actually uses it. → Section 7.
8. A punch list of **~35 copy/rendering defects** (garbled glyphs, icon-font failures, duplicated chips, typos, 2023/2024/2026 date drift, three different "Sarah" personas). → Section 8.

**Nothing here requires redesigning screens.** It requires: one sidebar master component per portal, a terminology sweep, deleting/merging the duplicates, adding the missing frames, and about eight rulings from Sue (listed in Section 6).
