## 1. What the worker workspace is (canon)

- **Persona:** self-employed / independent support workers. TMG180 gives them "everything a provider gives them — compliance, insurance tracking, verification, directory listing — without turning them into employees or taking a percentage of their income" (Gaps Analysis §8). The workspace exists to (1) produce evidence, (2) support participant choice, (3) maintain the platform boundary (Technical Brief §3).
- **Platform CAN:** present worker profiles for participant-directed choice; provide governance templates, tools and guidance; verify worker eligibility documents and access conditions; support longitudinal evidence structures. **MUST NOT:** assign/rank/match workers; supervise, direct or control a worker's service delivery; become the employer; own or control worker service records; let workers complete participant-facing inputs on their behalf (Technical Brief §4).
- **Three data layers, architecturally separated:** participant data (participant-owned, readable by *linked* workers with consent), **worker data (owned by the worker — their service records are their own, not platform-controlled)**, platform data (Technical Brief §7).
- **WCPS Layer A — worker private narrative** (`tmg_daily_note_private.private_narrative` / `safety_narrative`) is **NEVER** accessible to the participant, other workers or admin. Legal only. The shared structured layer (`tmg_daily_note_structured`) is what flows to the participant and their snapshot (DB doc §2, Master Map doc 20).
- **Consent gate:** a worker sees participant-owned information only while an active `tmg_consent` row for that worker exists; revocation takes effect immediately; "no queries with participant_id return data when no active consent record exists" (DB doc §3). Build Guide §5: *"Hard rule in middleware, not controllers: no worker-authenticated route writes to participant check-ins; worker reads require an active consent grant."*
- **R-07 (settled):** `workspace_access` is granted immediately on account creation; `directory_published` requires `onboarding_complete AND opt_in`. Pill copy: **"Complete onboarding to publish your profile to the directory — optional."** The old copy implying tools are locked is wrong — Worker Onboarding step 6 ("Access workspace tools") and the header sub-line on `1170:8393` must be rewritten to match.
- **Relational-first profile (P3-01…P3-04, WF-06):** the worker introduces themselves as a person before credentials. Seven prompts (verbatim from the seed): *A little about me · How I naturally support people · How I usually communicate · Where I do my best support work · Things I enjoy · What people often appreciate about working with me · The kind of working relationship that suits me best.* Résumé details (support areas, availability, location, languages, credentials, experience) are **supporting details**, rendered below/behind. Contact notice wherever contact details show: **"TMG180 does not coordinate services. Participants contact workers directly using their preferred contact method."** (v2 wording, `3233:23`).
- **Directory (P4-01…04, R-04, R-06):** cards lead with relational fields; **no ratings anywhere**; **availability never in the list payload** — only under `supporting_details.availability` in the profile detail response; alphabetical order; filters = Location + Support area. Strengths ("What I bring to support") are worker-authored, never feedback data.
- **Never revive the withdrawn worker-matching screens** (Handoff §3, D-06 archive page).
- **Incidents (Gaps §3):** workers have mandatory reporting obligations; a worker-facing incident report form / escalation pathway is *not specified* (backlog M-05). Don't invent it; the Governance Standing "Incident and Complaint Process" acknowledgement is what the frames show.
- **Invoices (Gaps §2/§3, DB §5):** `tmg_invoices` + audit exist in the DDL; workflow spec is unwritten (backlog M-06). Only the Worker Resource Detail frame's outlier sidebar mentions "Invoices" — not in the worker IA otherwise. Leave out.
- **Learning Hub content map** is unwritten (Gaps §4, MEDIUM) — module structure/order/completion tracking are not specced. The frames show the shape; content is Sue's.

## 2. Language rules that bite the worker screens

Banned everywhere: match/matching, tasks, assigned caseload, productivity, programs, clinical notes, ACTION REQUIRED, non-compliant, Care Management, Governance Admin. Participant-facing only: FCA, Baseline, Assessment. Approved replacements: "Needs review" (not Action Required), "Support overview" (not Performance Snapshots), "Coming up", "Manage your support event logs, view the participants you support (with consent), and connect with your support network" (worker workspace description), "Add a participant you support" (not Add Participant), "Engagement type" (not Contract type). Frames also fixed: "You have 4 sessions today", "Your workspace is taking shape" (not "Profile Completion 85%"), "Continue when you're ready" (not Complete Setup). Prefer first names in worker-facing views where consent allows, not "Participant #882" (Gaps §5).

**Hits still in the canonical frames (don't copy):** `1170:7686` Help Centre footer "PRIVACY & COMPLIANCE NOTICE … must comply with" → use "in line with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (APPs)"; `1169:3172` heading "Baseline comparison" (worker-facing, technically allowed — but our shared contract already renamed the concept "usual pattern"; recommend "Compared with their usual pattern"). Adjacent phrasing to avoid: `1170:8551` sidebar "WORKER MANAGEMENT" and "Invoices"; `1170:8393` step 6 "participant management" (caseload framing).

**Hits in the built code:** `worker/DailyLogForm.jsx:55-60,105,283-290` (`Better/Same/Below baseline`, "Baseline Comparison"), `:224` ("Select 1-3 FCA goals"), `worker/HelpCentre.jsx:139` ("Privacy & Compliance Notice"), `worker/LearningHubResource.jsx:139` ("ensuring compliance"), plus `:37` "shift".

## 4. Data — what exists vs what is missing

**Exists (Prisma, `apps/api/prisma/schema.prisma`)**
- `User` → `tmg_users`: `roles String[]` (participant/worker/admin), `status`, `full_name`, `email`, `ndis_number`; relations already named for the worker side (`notes_authored "WorkerNotes"`, `consents_held "WorkerConsents"`, `invoices_raised "WorkerInvoices"`). Sign-up as worker is live; a new worker gets full workspace access immediately (R-07-consistent).
- `Consent` → `tmg_consent`: `participant_id`, `worker_id?`, `consent_type`, `status` (`active|superseded|revoked` in our controller), `can_view_intake` (= "My Personal Profile" in participant copy), `can_view_snapshot`, `can_add_daily_note`, `can_view_checkins`, `superseded_by`; index `[worker_id, status]` — **the worker consent gate reads this**. Append-only, supersession + revocation already enforced in `privacy.controller.ts`. No grant flow yet (waits on the directory) — consents are seeded with SQL.
- `DailyNotePrivate` → `tmg_daily_note_private` (WCPS Layer A: `worker_id`, `participant_id`, session date/times, `service_type`, `location`, `private_narrative`, `safety_narrative`, `is_locked`) — **exists, nothing reads or writes it.**
- `DailyNoteStructured` → `tmg_daily_note_structured`: already carries `author_id` / `author_role` / nullable `worker_id` / `private_note_id` (the 2026-08-18 migration made one table serve both R-09 layers). Participant routes scope `author_role = 'participant'`; the worker layer writes `author_role = 'worker'`, `worker_id = me`, `participant_id = them`, `private_note_id → tmg_daily_note_private`. Fields: `goal_ids[]`, `domain_tags[]`, `impact_text`, `support_text`, `outcome_text`, `baseline_comparison` (internal name), `participant_voice`, `safety_note`, `status draft|submitted`, `submitted_at`, `is_locked`. `DailyNoteAddendum` has `added_by_role`.
- `ParticipantGoal` → derived from the profile's My Goals section on every `GET /participant/goals` (idempotent sync). The worker log needs the *same* goal rows for the consented participant.
- `MonthlySnapshot` (locked ones are what a worker with `can_view_snapshot` may read), `Notification` (`recipient_role`), `AuditLog` (`actor_role`), `Invoice`/`InvoiceAudit`, `ExternalWorkerInvitation`/`ExternalSupportNote` (Layer D — participant-driven, not this workspace).
- `packages/shared`: `ROLES`, `SELF_SIGNUP_ROLES`, `DAILY_LOG_AUTHOR_ROLE.WORKER` (unused), `FUNCTIONAL_DOMAINS` (nine, taken from `1169:3172`), `USUAL_PATTERN_COMPARISONS`, `canSubmitDailyLog`, `CONSENT_PERMISSIONS`, `consentSummary()`, `CONSENT_STATUS`, `SESSION_PREFERENCE_GROUPS` (support focus / availability / relational style vocab the participant filters on — the worker profile should share it).

## 5. API — what exists vs what the workspace needs

Exists: `/api/v1/auth/*`, `/participant/*` (profile, goals, daily-logs, snapshots, privacy, session-preferences), `/terminology`, and one worker-adjacent endpoint **`GET /user/worker/list`** (`user.controller.ts`) — returns *every* `tmg_users` row with the worker role, all columns except `password_hash` (emails, ndis_number, status…), to any authenticated user.
