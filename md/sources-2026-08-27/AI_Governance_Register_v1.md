TMG180 AI Governance Register
Version: 1.0
Status: AI Governance Control Register
Purpose
This register provides a consolidated governance overview of AI systems, AI-assisted workflows, and AI governance controls operating within the TMG180 ecosystem.
The purpose of this register is to:
support transparency;
support governance oversight;
support audit readiness;
support implementation consistency;
support responsible AI use;
and provide a central reference point for AI governance across the ecosystem.

AI Governance Principles
TMG180 utilises AI as assistive infrastructure.
AI systems exist to support:
workflow accessibility;
documentation support;
longitudinal understanding;
governance consistency;
operational sustainability;
participant-centred implementation;
and relational continuity.
AI systems do not replace:
participant autonomy;
human judgement;
professional responsibilities;
safeguarding obligations;
governance oversight;
or legal accountability.

AI Workflow Functions
AI may be utilised to support:
workflow guidance;
documentation assistance;
longitudinal summarisation;
operational navigation;
governance interpretation;
educational resources;
and implementation support.

AI Restrictions
AI must not:
replace safeguarding responsibilities;
make autonomous governance decisions;
remove participant choice and control;
replace professional judgement;
create hidden decision-making processes;
perform regulatory functions;
or operate without governance oversight.

Governance Principle
AI exists to strengthen human-centred support systems.
AI should reduce fragmentation, administrative burden, and operational complexity while preserving participant autonomy, worker sustainability, safeguarding, transparency, and governance integrity.

Governance Note
All AI systems operating within TMG180 remain subject to:
human oversight;
governance review;
safeguarding obligations;
privacy requirements;
and ongoing evaluation.
