TMG180 — Daily Relational Evidence Note
Participant-Owned Longitudinal Support Mapping
Canonical Template Document
Purpose
The TMG180 Daily Relational Evidence Note is designed to capture what support happened, what support was needed, what changed as a result, and how the support compared to the participant’s usual baseline.
These notes are not about judging, monitoring, or proving failure.
They exist to help build a respectful picture over time of:
what everyday life actually feels like
what support helps
how capacity fluctuates
what recovery costs look like
what becomes harder during stress, illness, overload, burnout, or change
and what support makes participation possible
The goal is not to remove participant voice.
The goal is to organise patterns over time so the participant’s experience becomes easier to understand, explain, and evidence when needed.

Core Principles
Daily notes must remain:
short
respectful
structured
low cognitive load
mobile-friendly
participant-owned
relationally grounded
focused on impact and support
A harder day does not mean failure.
Needing support does not mean the participant is doing something wrong.
Improvement with support does not mean support is no longer needed.
Capacity can fluctuate.
Support-mediated functioning is still evidence of support need.

Worker Note Guidance
Workers should write notes that are:
factual
respectful
proportionate
impact-focused
non-shaming
clear and brief
Avoid:
diagnostic speculation
assumptions about motivation
emotionally loaded wording
deficit-only framing
language that removes participant autonomy
Use:
“Participant appeared overwhelmed and unable to continue the task today.”
Instead of:
“Participant was non-compliant.”
Use:
“Participant became distressed during increased stress and required reduced demands and calm support.”
Instead of:
“Participant failed to regulate emotions.”

Developer Instructions
This daily note structure must remain linked to the intake baseline.
The required baseline hooks are:
Goal link
Which goal or support purpose did today’s support relate to?
Functional impact link
Which functional impacts were present today?
Baseline comparison
Was today typical, more supported, less supported, or different from baseline?
These fields are essential because they allow daily notes to build longitudinal evidence over time.
The daily note should remain:
short
repeatable
structured
mobile-friendly
low cognitive load
easy to complete consistently
The structure should support:
pattern detection
fluctuation mapping
recovery cost tracking
support effectiveness evidence
monthly summaries
AI-assisted evidence translation
participant-owned longitudinal records
The relational language sits on top of the structure.
The structure itself should not be removed or redesigned without preserving the baseline logic.
