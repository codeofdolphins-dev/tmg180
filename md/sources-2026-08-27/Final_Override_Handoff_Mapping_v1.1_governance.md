	| 6. Standing boundaries carried into this override
TMG180 does not coordinate services. Participants contact independent workers directly using the worker's preferred method.
No in-platform messaging between participants and workers.
No automated matching, ranking or recommendation of workers.
No processing of NDIS plan money.
AI produces drafts only — nothing is finalised without a human review gate.
Audit and role-change records are metadata only and append-only; no participant evidence content is written to them.
7. Handoff acceptance
Handoff is accepted when every row in section 2 passes its check, no banned term in section 4 appears in the interface, and the governance console language matches the approved trio. Any deviation is logged against the document register with a reason before sign-off.
