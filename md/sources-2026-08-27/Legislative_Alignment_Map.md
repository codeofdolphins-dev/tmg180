TMG180 Legislative Alignment Map
This map shows how each NDIS legislative provision and governance obligation anchors a specific TMG180 governance response, and the platform tools that produce auditable evidence of it. It is not legal advice. Source currency should be verified before formal reliance.
Read left to right: Legislation sets the obligation → Governance translates it into a standard or framework → Platform makes it visible and auditable.
Legislation / Principle
	| TMG180 Governance Response
	| Platform Evidence / Tools
	| NDIS Act s 3 – Objects
Independence, participation, meaningful life
	| Foundational precepts — people as whole human beings, not diagnoses or funding categories
	| Personal Profile, participant voice prompts, goal-linking tools
	| NDIS Act s 4 – General principles
Autonomy, dignity of risk, equal partnership
	| Choice and control framework — participant direction must be visible in selection, timing, and review
	| Participant check-ins, choice prompts, worker fit tools
	| NDIS Act s 5 – Acts on behalf of participants
Supported decision-making
	| Supported decision model — support helps the participant decide, not replace their decision
	| Participant portal, plain-language tools, profile prompts
	| NDIS Act s 7 – Communication
Accessible, understandable language
	| Plain-language standard — no provider-only or professional-only interpretation
	| Website access layers, NDIS knowledge library, AI output modes
	| NDIS Act s 17A – Capacity principles
Presume capacity, maximise choice and control
	| Relational discipline framework — power awareness, ethical fit, non-shaming accountability
	| Worker profiles, fit and reflection standards, transition pathways
	| NDIS Act s 31 – Plan preparation
Individualised, participant-directed, flexible
	| Goals / support needs / funded supports distinction — plan is a funding map, not a provider program
	| Goal Link Helper, monthly snapshot summaries, daily evidence logs
	| NDIS Act s 34 – Reasonable and necessary
Functional impact, goals, effectiveness, value
	| Support interpretation manual — links impairment, support need, goals, participation and evidence
	| Daily Support Evidence Logs, NDIS interpretation library
	| NDIS Code of Conduct
Dignity, safety, integrity, transparency, self-determination
	| Relational practice standards — non-shaming, boundaried, ethical, trauma-aware support
	| Incidents and complaints pathway, feedback tools, worker onboarding
	| NDIS Practice Standards
Informed choice, responsive support, participant involvement
	| ISO-aligned audit architecture — relational layer made visible and auditable (gap not covered by NDIS)
	| Governance domain register, audit pathway, policy library
	| Privacy Act / APPs / NDB
Data minimisation, privacy by design, breach notification
	| Platform and technical governance — role separation, data boundaries, participant-owned evidence
	| Privacy policy, cybersecurity policy, AI governance rules
	| WHS / Psychosocial risk
Worker safety, burnout prevention, emotional wellbeing
	| Worker sustainability model — operational infrastructure without becoming employer or directing delivery
	| Worker portal, onboarding tools, risk/WHS policy, workflow templates
	| Governance note: TMG180 does not treat human-centred practice as separate from governance. It treats human-centred practice as one of the things governance must protect and make visible. An auditor should be able to trace: principle → rationale → policy or standard → platform field or workflow → user action → evidence output → review pathway.
