TMG180 Governance Architecture
TMG180 Governance Architecture
Version: 1.0
Status: Draft Governance Structure
Purpose
This document defines the structure of the TMG180 governance suite and identifies the purpose of each core governance document.
The purpose of this architecture is to:
provide a clear governance framework structure;
reduce document duplication;
support audit readiness;
support implementation continuity;
support future document management;
and provide a central reference point for the TMG180 ecosystem.

Governance Suite Structure
Volume 1 – TMG180 Foundational Framework
Purpose:
Defines the philosophy, principles, purpose, and conceptual foundations of the TMG180 ecosystem.
Primary Audience:
Participants, workers, stakeholders, governance reviewers.

Volume 2 – Governance & Operations Manual
Purpose:
Defines mandatory governance controls, policies, operational requirements, safeguarding, privacy, complaints, incident management, and accountability structures.
Primary Audience:
Workers, auditors, governance reviewers, compliance stakeholders.

Volume 3 – Development & Integration Report
Purpose:
Documents the development journey, governance integration process, legislative alignment methodology, and ecosystem design rationale.
Primary Audience:
Auditors, legal reviewers, governance reviewers, future implementation teams.

Volume 4 – Relational Governance Practice Standards Manual
Purpose:
Defines relational governance standards, best-practice guidance, ethical frameworks, and worker practice expectations.
Primary Audience:
Workers, governance reviewers, training and onboarding functions.

Volume 5 – NDIS Interpretation & Knowledge Library
Purpose:
Provides educational resources, support interpretation guidance, participant-centred practice information, and operational learning materials.
Primary Audience:
Workers, participants, support professionals.

Volume 6 – Platform & Technical Governance Manual
Purpose:
Defines platform operations, technical governance, platform boundaries, workflow architecture, permissions, and digital infrastructure.
Primary Audience:
Developers, governance reviewers, implementation teams.

Governance Principle
The TMG180 governance suite is designed to ensure that governance, safeguarding, participant autonomy, worker sustainability, relational practice, legislative alignment, and digital infrastructure remain integrated within a coherent operational ecosystem.
