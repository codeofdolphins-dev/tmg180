How These Templates Work
These three templates work together to build participant-owned longitudinal evidence over time. They sit inside the TMG180 platform and connect automatically — but they can also be used as standalone documents.

Template A — Support Event Log (Worker)
Completed by the worker after each visit. Takes 30–60 seconds.
Captures what support happened, what it helped with, and how the participant was today.

Template B — Participant Check-in
Completed by the participant — weekly, after a support period, or whenever they choose.
Captures their own experience in their own words.

Template C — Monthly Snapshot Summary
Generated from Template A and B logs each month.
Reviewed and approved by the participant before it is locked.
This becomes the exportable evidence document for assessments.
 | 

Tone rules — non-negotiable:
  • Use neutral, non-shaming language at all times
  • Capacity can fluctuate — a harder week does not mean going backwards
  • A lower rating is not a failure — it is information
  • Improvements reflect supports working, not removal of impairment
  • Worker narrative notes remain confidential — only structured tags are shared
