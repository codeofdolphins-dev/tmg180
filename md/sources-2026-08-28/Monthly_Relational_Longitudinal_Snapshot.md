TMG180 — Monthly Relational Longitudinal Snapshot
Participant-Owned Longitudinal Support Mapping
Canonical Monthly Summary Template
Purpose
The TMG180 Monthly Relational Longitudinal Snapshot is designed to create a clearer picture over time of:
how support is affecting everyday life
what has become more manageable or sustainable
what continues to create barriers
what support is helping
how capacity fluctuates
and what recovery and participation are actually costing the participant over time
This summary is not intended to:
judge progress,
measure worth,
or reduce people to symptoms or compliance outcomes.
The purpose is to help:
preserve participant voice
build longitudinal understanding
reduce repeated re-explaining
support reassessment evidence
and demonstrate the relationship between support and sustainable participation over time.
This is a participant-owned summary.
A harder month does not mean failure.
Fluctuation does not automatically mean regression.
Improvement with support reflects support working — not removal of impairment.

Section 10 — Non-Linear Functioning Statement
Important Context
Capacity can fluctuate.
A harder period does not automatically mean regression.
Improvement with support reflects support working.
Many participants experience:
fluctuating capacity,
changing recovery needs,
varying stress tolerance,
and periods of increased support need depending on health, environment, stress, burnout, sensory load, or life circumstances.
This is consistent with many psychosocial, neurodivergent, trauma-related, chronic illness, and invisible disability experiences.
Suggested longitudinal wording:
“Capacity varied across the month in line with non-linear functioning patterns. Participation improved with appropriate support scaffolding, pacing, and relational support. Improvements reflected support effectiveness and reduced recovery cost rather than removal of underlying impairment.”
